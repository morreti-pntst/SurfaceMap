# Commercial licensing

The [SurfaceMap Community License v1.0](LICENSE) allows you to use SurfaceMap for paid penetration testing, security audits, consulting, and training. Section 3 covers selling the software, paid distribution, product bundling that monetizes its functionality, and paid access to hosted instances.

## Request permission

Contact **[Commercial licensing contact to be supplied]** for **[Legal name and contact details of the authorized rights holder or representative to be supplied]**. Include your organization's legal name, the intended product or service, the versions and components involved, who would receive copies or access, and how you plan to charge.

Discuss a fixed fee, periodic fees, an agreed share of revenue, or a combination. Rates and obligations are set case by case. No percentage or fee applies automatically.

A separate signed agreement should specify the parties, covered versions, allowed uses and distribution, remuneration and calculation base, reporting and verification rights, payment dates, term, governing law, and dispute resolution. Any permissions for third-party components must come from their respective rights holders.

Begin the requested activity only after the agreement has been signed by you and the parties authorized to grant the relevant rights.
